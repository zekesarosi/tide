Direction B

Calm Modern

The Slack Water design system taken to sea. The app answers first, and one glanceable sheet carries what matters right now. The map is quiet: depth is shown as soft tone, not linework, and detail arrives as you zoom. Guidance reads like Apple Maps turn-by-turn (“Hold course to Mira Head”). Tide and current are woven in as the same drift trails and tide line the app already uses. Native iOS surfaces do the heavy lifting: sheets, Live Activities, the Lock Screen and the Watch.

Typography

6.4 kn  Hold course

  • SF Pro (shown here as Inter) for text: 22 bold for guidance, 17 for body, and 13 semibold caps for section labels.
  • SF Pro Rounded (shown here as Nunito) Light for readings at 46 pt, as in Font.reading.
  • Sentences, not abbreviations: “Hold course to Mira Head”, “Never less than 3.8 m under your keel”.

Iconography

anchorwindcurrentrouteaismoblayersbelldepthcompass

SF Symbols wherever one exists (location.north, wind, water.waves, sailboat). Custom marine glyphs are drawn to match: 2 pt rounded strokes on the 24 grid. On the map, own boat is a puck, not a hull, and AIS targets are small rounded triangles in neutral grey until they matter.

Sunlight readability

  • The map stays pale, and the numbers sit on opaque sheets, never on glass, when the ambient light sensor reads full sun. Glass materials go opaque automatically above 50k lux.
  • The thin Rounded weight steps up to Regular when Increase Contrast is on or in bright light.
  • Ink #0D2230 on white is 15:1, and secondary text is 6:1, so Slack Water’s contrast is kept.

Wet and gloved hands

  • Every target is at least 52 pt, primary buttons are 56 pt, and the floating controls are 52×52.
  • The sheet has three fixed detents, so a wet swipe can’t land halfway.
  • The Action button and the Watch crown give MOB and Mark. A water-lock mode ignores touches until the crown is pressed.
  • Destructive actions are press-and-hold with a haptic tick.
