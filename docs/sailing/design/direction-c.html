Sailor’s Almanac

A cruising guide and a tide almanac that happen to know where you are. It leads with what this app does better than any plotter: tide, stream and surge, predicted to the millimetre. It is laid out like a well-set page, with serif headlines, small-caps labels and paper-chart linework. Every screen shares one signature control, the time scrubber. Drag it and the chart’s streams, wind and depths move with it. Passages are planned on a time–distance chart, so tidal gates read at a glance.

Typography

Sandy Cove
SOG · DEPTH · TIDES  HW 06:36 2.0

  • Newsreader (serif, optical sizes) for headlines, readings and pilot prose, from 15 to 72 pt.
  • IBM Plex Sans Condensed semibold small caps for labels and chart names.
  • IBM Plex Mono for tide tables and logs, so the columns line up like an almanac.

Iconography

anchorwindcurrentrouteaismoblayersbelldepthcompass

Fine 1.4 pt engraved strokes with butt caps, as on a printed chart. The chart symbols follow INT 1 / US Chart No. 1 literally: tidal diamonds, feathered stream arrows, the anchorage anchor and the magenta course line. UI icons are kept to a minimum. Words and small caps do most of the labelling.

Sunlight readability

  • Warm paper (#F4EFE3) glares less than pure white, and ink stays at 14:1 against it.
  • Information is ranked by type size and weight rather than by colour, so it survives a washed-out screen.
  • The chart linework is thicker than on paper (contours 0.7 pt, coast 1.1 pt), and soundings are 9 pt italic.
  • A “Bright sun” toggle drops the paper tint to white and doubles the rule weights.

Wet and gloved hands

  • The scrubber handle has a 48×44 pt hit area. Coarse drags snap to 15 min, and a tap on the hour labels jumps there.
  • Primary buttons are 58 pt tall, full-width pairs with 10 pt gaps.
  • Reading screens are scroll-free while underway. Scrolling prose appears only at anchor or in planning.
  • Ending the anchor watch is a 2 s hold, and Silence auto-expires.
